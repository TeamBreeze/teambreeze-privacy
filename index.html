About this policy

This policy covers the current version of TeamBreeze for iPhone and iPad. The app manages groups and people, creates teams and randomly picks people who are present. It processes group records locally on your device. Voluntary messages and beta testing involve the additional data flows explained below.

Data stored on your device

Stored records include group names and order, group symbols or emoji and symbol colours, people’s names, attendance and internal person IDs. You may optionally add gender, ability level and rules for keeping people together or apart. The most recently used team settings and whether person rules are applied are also saved for each group.

These records are used only to manage groups and attendance, create teams locally and pick a person. TeamBreeze does not send the stored group records to Mika Hoge or an app server. There is no TeamBreeze account, advertising, tracking or integrated analytics or crash-reporting service. Mika Hoge does not sell the data and cannot remotely access your group records.

Responsibility for the people you enter

Anyone using TeamBreeze with other people’s data determines the purpose and scope of that use. Where the GDPR applies, the responsible person or organisation must have an appropriate legal basis, provide the required information and honour data subjects’ rights. Enter only necessary details; suitable aliases may replace names. Local processing alone does not make every use legally permissible.

Protection on your device

Group records are encrypted locally using AES-GCM. The 256-bit key is held in the device-bound keychain, is accessible while the device is unlocked and is not synchronised with iCloud. The data file uses complete iOS file protection. The app does not save an unencrypted temporary file; data needed during use is decrypted in memory.

When the app becomes inactive, it covers its interface in the app switcher and its external team view. Third-party keyboard extensions are disabled in TeamBreeze. There is no additional Face ID, Touch ID or passcode prompt. Someone who can use your unlocked device can therefore also use the open app. Use your device lock and protect your device against unauthorised access.

Storage, changes and deletion

Groups and people remain stored on your device until you delete them in the app; there is no automatic expiry. You can change names and details at any time and delete people or groups after confirmation. Associated saved person rules or group settings are also removed. The app does not store a history of these edits.

Generated teams and the undo history stay in memory only while the team creation view is open. The result of a random pick is not saved permanently either. Saved team settings are retained separately. Mika Hoge cannot read, delete or restore your local records; for records entered by someone else, contact the person or organisation that entered them.

Backups and changing devices

The encrypted data file is marked for exclusion from regular system backups. The operating system does not guarantee exclusion from every backup. The key is bound to this device. There is currently no iCloud sync, export or restoration on another device. Losing your device or deleting the app may permanently lose your groups. Deleting records in the app does not retrospectively change existing device backups.

Connected displays

If you connect a supported external display through the system using AirPlay or a cable, TeamBreeze can automatically show the current teams there, including team numbers and people’s names. Gender, ability level and person rules are not shown. You set up the display connection yourself; people in front of the display can see the names. Without a current team assignment, or after team creation is closed, the external team view is cleared. The app does not upload group records to a developer server for this.

Voluntary emails and feedback

“Send feedback” opens your default email app with a recipient address and subject. TeamBreeze does not add group or person records, device information, logs or attachments, and does not send the message itself. If you explicitly copy the contact address, it is written to the system clipboard.

Only when you send the message does Mika Hoge receive your sender address, your name if included, message content, email metadata and any attachments you add. These are used to handle your enquiry and understand errors or suggestions, not for advertising. General enquiries are processed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in communication and improving the app. Enquiries relating to a contract or pre-contractual steps with you are processed under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Contact is voluntary and is not required to use the app. A reply may not be possible without a reachable return address. Please do not send personal group data or screenshots containing it. Use fictitious names and a description of the problem wherever possible.

Email providers and transfers

The recipient mailbox teambreeze@outlook.de is a personal Outlook.com/Outlook.de mailbox provided by Microsoft. Your own email provider and the mail servers involved also take part in delivery and receive the message and connection data needed for it. Microsoft generally identifies Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited as controller for its services in the European Economic Area. Processing by Microsoft and its service providers may also take place outside the EEA, particularly in the USA.

This consumer mailbox is subject to the Microsoft Services Agreement and Microsoft Privacy Statement. Microsoft describes safeguards for transfers outside the EEA, including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers to certified US companies and standard contractual clauses. Details of recipients, safeguards and how to obtain a copy are in the Microsoft privacy statement. Storage exclusively in Europe is not promised.

TestFlight beta testing

For a TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects crash and usage data and shares it with the developer. This may include device and version information, sessions and, depending on the invitation, name and email address. Submitted feedback may include comments and screenshots. TestFlight does not offer an opt-out from this collection. Beta data is used to fix and improve the app.

Mika Hoge evaluates this data under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in running a safe and functional beta test. Beta data is not used for advertising or shared with third parties. This additional processing relates to TestFlight distribution, not an analytics service integrated into TeamBreeze. Apple’s own processing and retention are also subject to its linked TestFlight privacy notice. The App Store and operating system process data under Apple’s own terms.

Retention of messages and beta data

Mika Hoge retains support messages and locally saved beta feedback only for as long as needed to handle the enquiry or fix the reported problem. They are then deleted or used without personal identifiers. If legal retention duties or the establishment or defence of claims require longer storage, the necessary data is kept until those duties or periods expire and restricted to that purpose. Legal obligations are based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR, and legal claims on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Beta data available in Apple’s services and any remaining backup or recovery copies held by email providers are also subject to those providers’ retention and deletion procedures. Deleting a message from an inbox therefore does not mean that all provider copies are immediately erased.

Your privacy rights

Where the legal requirements are met, you have rights regarding data processed by Mika Hoge: access (Article 15 GDPR), rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction (Article 18) and portability (Article 20). Contact teambreeze@outlook.de to exercise them. You may withdraw any consent you have given with effect for the future, without affecting earlier processing. The general support and beta evaluation described here are not based on consent.

You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (Article 21 GDPR). Processing will then stop unless overriding compelling legitimate grounds exist or the data is needed for legal claims.

You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR), in particular where you habitually live, work or where the alleged infringement occurred. For person records held only locally, contact the person or organisation using them in TeamBreeze. The app does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects; the user remains responsible for applying a suggested team assignment.
